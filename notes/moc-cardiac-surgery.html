心臟外科索引 · Cardiac Surgery MOC

一眼主軸

先定位要修的是 coronary、valve、aorta、pump 或 congenital anatomy，再把手術前後接回血行動力學：preload、afterload、contractility、rhythm 與 bleeding。

術後五問

灌流夠不夠？　有沒有在流血？　心包有沒有壓迫？ 節律與 pacing 是否穩定？　肺、腎與神經功能有沒有偏離？

一眼骨架

Cardiac surgery
├─ Coronary：CAD／ACS → CABG
├─ Valve：stenosis／regurgitation → repair／replacement
├─ Aorta：dissection／aneurysm → aortic repair
├─ Pump：advanced HF／shock → mechanical support／transplant
└─ Congenital：TOF／d-TGA／pulmonary atresia → 支持／橋接／個別重建

五角度專篇索引

Disease

  • CAD · ACS — coronary disease
  • Aortic dissection — aortic emergency
  • Infective endocarditis — 感染與瓣膜破壞
  • Heart failure · Cardiac tamponade · Atrial fibrillation — perioperative hemodynamics／rhythm
  • Aortic stenosis↗ · Mitral regurgitation↗ — 待寫
  • TOF — 四項解剖異常、肺血流與發紺
  • TGA · Pulmonary atresia — 詳見 Pediatric heart disease

Test

  • Echocardiography · Cardiac auscultation · Troponin · EKG ischemia
  • Coronary angiography↗ · Right heart catheterization↗ — 待寫

Drug

  • Antiplatelet · DOAC · Statin · Antiarrhythmics

Intervention

  • CABG↗ · Valve repair↗ · Valve replacement↗ · Aortic repair↗ — 待寫
  • ECMO · ICD · Chest tube drainage
  • TOF correction — 完整矯正、術前支持、tet spell、shunt／stent 橋接
  • TGA correction — BAS、arterial switch、atrial switch 與複雜型替代重建
  • Pulmonary atresia intervention — PA/IVS、PA/VSD 與 MAPCAs 的導管／手術路徑
  • Cardiopulmonary bypass↗ · Temporary epicardial pacing↗ — 待寫

Workup

  • Postcardiotomy low cardiac output↗ · Postoperative bleeding↗ · New postoperative atrial fibrillation↗ — 待寫
  • Dyspnea · Chest pain↗ · Shock
